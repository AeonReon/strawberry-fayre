Our Story

A Proper Tea Room — Where Everything Is Made from Scratch

Strawberry Fayre isn't your typical cafe. Tucked away on the Blagh Road just outside Coleraine, we've been welcoming locals and visitors with simple, honest, home-cooked food — the kind your granny would be proud of.

No shortcuts, no frozen packets, no pretension. Just fresh soups, generous sandwiches, warm scones with real butter, and a traybake selection that'll have you taking half of it home. Our regulars have been coming back for years, and we treat every visitor like one of the family.

Pull in, grab a seat by the window, and let us look after you.

What We Serve

Home-Cooked Food That Keeps People Coming Back

From hearty lunches to legendary afternoon tea — everything's made fresh daily, right here in our kitchen.

Soups & Sandwiches

Homemade soup of the day served with fresh bread, plus generous sandwiches and filled rolls. Prawn sandwiches are a firm favourite.

Hot Lunches

Proper home-cooked mains with generous portions — Irish pizzas, braised steak, pineapple chicken, and more. Wholesome food, no gimmicks.

Scones & Traybakes

Our famous cherry scones, apple pie, coconut cookies, chocolate cake and a full traybake counter you can pick from. Take some home — you'll want to.

Afternoon Tea

Settle in with a large pot of tea, warm scones with butter and jam, and a selection from our traybake display. The perfect afternoon out.

Tea & Coffee

"Best milky coffee in Northern Ireland" according to one of our regulars. Fresh brews, large pots of tea, and hot chocolate too.

Take-Away Bakes

Can't stay? Grab a box from the traybake counter. Cakes, scones, biscuits and more — all baked on-site and ready to take home.
